About me

The story so far

My path isn't a straight line, and I've come to see that as my edge. I started out studying criminology and anthropology at the University of Zululand, drawn to one big question: why do people and systems behave the way they do?

My first hands-on experience came at the Department of Correctional Services in Durban, where I worked on case assessments and rehabilitation programmes. It taught me to be careful with records, fair with people, and patient with process.

In 2022 I moved toward data. I trained in data science with Explore Data Science Academy, did fieldwork as a research data specialist in KwaZulu-Natal, and interned at BCX in digital insights and automation.

That mix led me to BCX's learning team in 2023, where I became the person behind SAP SuccessFactors Learning for thousands of colleagues. I love making complicated systems simple for the people who use them.

Next, I want to keep growing as an SAP learning specialist: configuring, supporting and improving learning systems, and using AI and analytics to make learning faster, clearer and more personal.
